The map

  • Nebula: a place (the repo); thicker with more tool calls there
  • Star: a session, coloured by its state
  • Planet on a ring: a subagent; fast when active, slow and dark when resting, bigger with more tool calls, a ring when finished

Stars (state)

  • busy: bright yellow-white
  • idle: dim orange
  • needs you: pulses cyan with beacon rings; a "?" above it for a question, a padlock for a permission prompt
  • offline: a faded ember
  • stalled: flickers, label warning sign (subagent planets too, with a red haze)
  • a ring bursting off a star or a planet flashing: a new tool call

Actions (on the star, in miniature on planets)

  • edit: flares leap off the surface
  • run: a pulsing corona
  • read: a sweeping beam
  • web: jets from both poles
  • delegate: its orbits glow gold
  • think: a steady breathing glow
  • other: a shimmer of sparkles
